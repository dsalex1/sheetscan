Sheetscan

You get an editable score to check against the original, play back, correct, and download as MusicXML (Finale, Sibelius, capella, MuseScore, Dorico) or MIDI.
Everything runs on your device; your music is not uploaded. The first scan downloads the recognition models (about 150 MB, kept for next time).

Free software (AGPL-3.0): source and accuracy tests. Recognition by homr, engraving by Verovio.
